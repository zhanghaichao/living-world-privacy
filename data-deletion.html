DATA CONTROL

Delete your Living World data.

Living World does not create a developer account or keep your data on a developer-operated server. You can remove data directly from your Android device.

1

Location & weather cache

Open Living World → Settings → Weather → Clear location & weather data. This also turns off automatic weather. You can separately revoke location permission in Android Settings → Apps → Living World → Permissions.

2

AI provider credentials

Open Living World → Settings → AI provider settings, then clear the saved configuration. This removes the encrypted endpoint, model names, and API keys from your device. Contact your chosen provider for deletion of data it processed.

3

Custom worlds & images

Open My Worlds, select a creation, and choose Remove. Exported copies are controlled by the destination you selected and must be deleted there separately.

4

Delete all local app data

Use Android Settings → Apps → Living World → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall Living World. Android will remove the app’s local settings, caches, credentials, and creations.

5

Google Play subscription data

Subscriptions belong to your Google Play account and are not cancelled by clearing app data. Open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to manage or cancel them. Use Google’s privacy controls for Google-held data.

NEED HELP?

There is no server-side Living World account to delete.
